Book overview

A gentler way to bring your ideas to life.

With short reflections, creative prompts, and practical exercises, this book helps you move from “what if?” to your very first small step. Pick it up anywhere and begin where you are.

01

Notice the ordinary

Find fresh questions and inspiration in the details you usually pass by.

02

Make a little space

Use simple routines to protect time for ideas without waiting for a perfect day.

03

Try, learn, repeat

Turn a big ambition into small experiments that are easier to begin.

Meet the author

Alex Rowan

Alex Rowan is a writer and workshop facilitator who helps teams and individuals make room for creative thinking. Their work is rooted in curiosity, reflection, and the belief that small ideas can lead somewhere wonderful.
